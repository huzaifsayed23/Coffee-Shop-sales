#Project Overview

The Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard is a data analytics project created to analyze customer purchasing behavior, sales trends, product performance, and store performance using raw coffee shop sales data.

This project focuses on transforming raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and KPI analysis.

# #Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance
Identify peak sales hours and customer footfall trends
Understand product category contribution to revenue
Compare store location performance
Discover top-selling products
Visualize weekday and customer ordering patterns

## Key KPIs
Total Sales: $698,812.33
Total Footfall: 149,116
Average Bill per Person: 4.69
Average Orders per Person: 1.44

## Insights Generated
Morning hours generated the highest quantity of orders
Coffee products contributed the largest share of total sales
Regular and Large drink sizes were the most preferred by customers
Hell’s Kitchen store achieved strong overall sales performance
Barista Espresso emerged as the top-selling product
Weekday sales were higher compared to weekends
